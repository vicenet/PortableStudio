# Privacy

PortableStudio runs on your computer inside Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop. It has no account, no analytics and
no tracking.

- Your designs, brand profiles, logos and generated files stay on your computer.
- Once a day it asks GitHub (api.github.com) whether a newer version exists, so it can tell you about
  updates. The request contains no personal data. Turn it off in the panel's settings.
- Adobe Exchange and GitHub, where you download PortableStudio, have their own privacy policies.

Questions: https://github.com/vicenet/PortableStudio/issues

If you turn on AI copywriting, the brand details you enter are sent to Anthropic's API with your own API key,
under Anthropic's terms. Licence activation (off by default) contacts the licence provider you configure.
