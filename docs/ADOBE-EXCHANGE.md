# Adobe Exchange listing: PortableStudio

Everything needed to publish PortableStudio on Adobe Exchange, where Illustrator and Photoshop users look
for plugins.

## Listing text

**Name:** PortableStudio

**Short description (one line):**
Free brand guidelines generator for Illustrator and Photoshop: brand books, logo presentations, carousels and logo kits.

**Description:**
PortableStudio turns a client's brand (name, colours, fonts, logos, photos and copy) into finished,
editable design files in minutes.

- A 43-page brand guidelines template: logo usage, construction grid, clearspace, misuse, colour palette (HEX/RGB/CMYK), typography, pattern, stationery and applications.
- A logo presentation for client pitches and a five-slide Instagram carousel.
- A complete logo export kit: SVG, EPS, PDF and PNG in every colour version, favicons and social images.
- Logo construction grids, palette tools and WCAG contrast checks.
- Turn your own Illustrator or Photoshop designs into templates with simple tags.
- Update a generated document after brand changes without losing your edits.
- Brand profiles, batch export, template packs, a local REST API and an MCP server for automation.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0). Starter templates are CC0, so they can be used in client work.

**Keywords:** brand guidelines, brand book, branding, logo presentation, logo kit, style guide, brand identity, template, instagram carousel, logo grid, colour palette, mockup

**Category:** Templates / Productivity (whichever the portal offers closest)

**Release notes:** see https://github.com/vicenet/PortableStudio/releases

## How to submit

1. Sign in at **developer.adobe.com** with your Adobe ID and open **Distribute** (Adobe Developer Distribution).
2. Create a new listing for a **CEP extension**, and upload the signed `PortableStudio-<version>.zxp` from the
   latest release (built by `npm run package`).
3. Paste the text below, upload the icon and screenshots from `.github/exchange/`, and choose the hosts
   (Illustrator and Photoshop, 2021 and later).
4. Choose **Free**, add the support and privacy links, and submit for review. Adobe reviews listings
   before they go live, which can take a few days to a few weeks.

Adobe's exact requirements (image sizes, text limits) change from time to time: the portal shows them
next to each field. Resize the images in `.github/exchange/` if it asks for different sizes.
